📦 Jılmay Cargo

Shop anywhere. We bring it home to Irad.

International shops don't deliver to Irad. Jılmay Cargo does. Order from any online store that ships to Kyrgyzstan, send it to your personal Jılmay address in Bishkek, and we'll truck it across the border to your door. Paid in US dollars only.

How it works

  1. 🏷️Get your address

    Log in to Jılmay and receive a Bishkek warehouse address with your personal code (JL-xxxxx).

  2. 🛒Shop online

    Order from any shop that delivers to Kyrgyzstan. Use your Jılmay code as the second address line.

  3. 🔎We check and pack

    Our Bishkek team weighs your parcel, photographs it and prepares the customs papers.

  4. 🚚Delivered to Irad

    Bonded trucks of Trans-Chuy Freight & Logistics bring it to your city in 5–9 days.
